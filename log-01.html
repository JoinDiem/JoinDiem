03 "Problem: traditional calendars make me feel guilty for running behind schedule"

I used to be a perfectionist with my calendar. I planned every day, every hour, every 15 minute interval. If I’m being completely honest, every single minute of the day. I thought it was productivity maxing, but it actually was not sustainable. I was burning out and constantly feeling like I wasn’t doing enough of my goals.

04 "Solution: calendar that flows naturally with life. good vibes only."

Why not create a calendar that flows naturally with you and doesn't shame you for the day.
